Dear Parent / Guardian,

Department of Science and Technology has proposed an INSPIRE award for innovative ideas in the field of science for environmental betterment.

The ideas have to be original, new and should not be copied from internet or the earlier innovations.

A small synopsis of the idea in less than 400 words should be submitted as hard copy and soft copy in a pendrive and short-listed students should prepare a working model of their submitted idea.

Interested students of Std VI and Std VII can submit their proposal to their Science teacher on or before 28th of July 2018.

For further details visit the site : http://www.inspireawards-dst.gov.in/
